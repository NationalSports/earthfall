# Earthfall: Frontline

The third-person tower-defense shooter that goes with Earthfall. See `HANDOFF.md` for the full brief.

**Status: milestone 1, greybox.** Open rolling ground north of Los Angeles: the swarm arrives across a 110 degree arc over the hills. A soldier, one enemy type (plus a larger one from wave 2), three turrets, three waves, win and lose screens.

- Build: `node frontline/build.js` (needs `three@0.128` installed at the repo root).
- Play: open `frontline/dist/index.html` in a desktop browser.
- Controls: W A S D move, mouse aim, click to fire, R reload, Shift sprint, Q and E turn.

Code lives in `frontline/src/` (Claude). Art will live in `frontline/assets/` (ChatGPT).
