# Frontline Mod

A short first-person mission in the browser. The port's security net has gone feral. Cut its three uplinks and reach the boat.

## Play

Open `frontline/frontline-mod/index.html` in a desktop browser, or run `node build.js` from `frontline/` and open `frontline/dist/frontline-mod.html`.

Click the page, then Deploy. W A S D move, mouse looks, click fires, right click aims, R reloads, E cuts a relay, Shift sprints, C crouches, Esc pauses.

The page bundles three.js. Models, textures, and sound are generated in the browser. The title uses fonts from Google Fonts, with a plain fallback if those do not load.
