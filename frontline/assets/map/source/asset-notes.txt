EARTHFALL: FRONTLINE / TACTICAL MAP ART

26 production assets, matching the names and dimensions in MAP_ART_BRIEF.md.
17 icons are white with #05090f outlines and real transparent padding/cutouts.
The 3 elevation ramps are opaque RGB lookup data, with low ground on the left.
The grain is RGBA with approximately 6–12% alpha, including thin 4px scanlines.
The frame has a fully transparent center, with decoration confined to its border.

TINT / COMPOSITION
Gold #e8be60: player, squad, supplies, objectives.
Red #ff4a6a: enemies and incoming strikes.
Purple #b04adf: nests.
Green #60ff90: extraction.
Tint white glyph pixels while preserving their dark outlines where supported.
Do not render the PNGs on opaque backplates before tinting.

Details are deliberately pale neutral tiles: multiply over the elevation ramp.
Use repeating/wrapped UVs. Periodic-plus-smooth processing removes edge drift;
a narrow periodic edge strip is reconciled before JPEG export.
The textures have approximately 2.8/255 channel standard deviation, so their
microdetail stays behind live symbols and labels.
The blue-grey grain adds the faint holographic sheen over #05090f.
The frame belongs in screen space, above terrain, below live interactive labels.
Panel text and mission information are supplied by code; none is baked into it.

ORIGINAL ART / PROVENANCE
Icons are original SVG geometry in this directory. The export helper uses sharp
at 4x resolution, Pillow for silhouette outlines, then Lanczos reduction.
No external icon libraries, game logos, game screenshots or reference art used.
Terrain bases were created with the built-in imagegen tool, then resized,
made periodic and normalized for low-contrast multiplicative blending.
Exact prompts are retained in imagegen-prompts.json.
